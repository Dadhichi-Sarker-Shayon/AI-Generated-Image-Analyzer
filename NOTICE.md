# Acknowledgements and third-party material

This project builds on the work of others. Thank you.

**Datasets** (not redistributed here; downloaded by `scripts/fetch_hf_datasets.py`)

- *Tiny-GenImage* — `TheKernel01/Tiny-GenImage` on Hugging Face (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), a subset of
  GenImage: Zhu et al., "GenImage: A Million-Scale Benchmark for Detecting AI-Generated Image", NeurIPS 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks.
- *Defactify Image Dataset* — Roy et al., "A Comprehensive Dataset for Human vs. AI Generated Image Detection" (arXiv:2601.00553);
  `Rajarshi-Roy-research/Defactify_Image_Dataset`. Real images from MS COCO (Lin et al., 2014).
- *Synthbuster / Synthbuster-plus* — Bammey, "Synthbuster: Towards Detection of Diffusion Model Generated Images" (IEEE OJSP 2023);
  `marco-willi/synthbuster-plus`. Real images from the RAISE raw-image dataset (Dang-Nguyen et al., 2015).

**Models and libraries**

- EfficientNet-B0 (Tan & Le, 2019) via [timm](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models) (Apache-2.0); ImageNet-pretrained initialization.
- BLIP image captioning (Li et al., 2022), `Salesforce/blip-image-captioning-base` (BSD-3-Clause) — optional, downloaded on demand.
- ONNX Runtime, PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, OpenCV, scikit-image, Pillow, pydantic.

No dataset images are included in this repository. Images generated by third-party services remain subject to those services' terms.
